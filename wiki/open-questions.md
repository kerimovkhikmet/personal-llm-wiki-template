Dated log of contradictions, gaps, or unresolved questions surfaced while ingesting or linking wiki pages. Don't fill a gap from memory dressed as a sourced fact - file it here instead and say so.

Each entry: what's unresolved, which pages/sources it touches, and what would resolve it (e.g. "drop a source into `raw/notes/` that covers X").

Entries are appended, newest at the bottom. Never prepend entries to the top, and never delete or reorder existing ones - resolve a gap with a new entry instead.
