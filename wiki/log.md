# Wiki log

This is the wiki's own changelog - distinct from `raw/`'s daily capture log. Append a dated entry after every ingest or structural change; entries are appended, newest at the bottom. Never prepend entries to the top or reorder existing ones.

Two line formats:

- `## [YYYY-MM-DD] ingest | raw/<path> + main changes` - a raw entry or clipping was ingested into the wiki.
- `## [YYYY-MM-DD] structure | short description` - a structural change (new page-type folder, reorganization, index/log format change) with no single raw source behind it.

Keep the body concise. If an entry covers several distinct changes, list them as `-` bullets, one per change; otherwise write a short sentence or two. Use bullets sparingly, only when a list reads better than prose.
